About me

A journey that starts
with my hands and ends in code.

I'm Tommaso, an IT technician and developer based near Milan, Italy. My path started in electrical work — a year as an electrician taught me to think in terms of systems, reliability and safety, skills I carry every day into networks and IT infrastructure. I currently work as an IT technician at MAST Srl, handling everything from helpdesk support to network installations, cybersecurity and firewall management. Alongside that, I'm co-founder and technical director of Easydomotica, a home-automation company I'm building with a friend and business partner since March 2024. In my (very limited) spare time I program for fun, I'm developing a video game in Unreal Engine 5, and I love photography.
