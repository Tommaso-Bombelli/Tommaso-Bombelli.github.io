About me

A journey that starts
with my hands.

I'm Tommaso, an IT technician and developer based near Milan. My path started in electrical work — a year as an electrician taught me to think in systems, reliability and safety, skills I carry into every network and infrastructure I touch today.

I currently work as an IT technician at MAST Srl, handling everything from helpdesk to network installation, firewall configuration and cybersecurity. Alongside that, I'm co-founder and technical director of Easydomotica, a home-automation company I'm building with my business partner since March 2024.

In whatever spare time is left: building a video game in Unreal Engine 5, maintaining a self-hosted homelab, and shooting photos.
