For businesses

The goal is simple: IT that doesn't stop, and a fast recovery when something breaks.

Free IT check-up

We look at computers, network, backup and security, and say what the company is risking. It's an assessment, not a sales pitch.

Managed support

A fixed yearly price per device. We monitor and update computers and servers remotely and step in before a problem stops the work.

Accounts, mail and Microsoft 365

Mailboxes and user accounts for every employee, set up when someone joins and closed when they leave.

Servers and domain controller

One login per person for their PC, shared folders and programs, with Active Directory and virtualised servers.

NAS and shared folders

A central file archive in the office, with permissions per person: accounting sees its folders, nobody else does.

Network and Wi-Fi

Router, firewall, switches and access points configured to be fast, stable and protected.

Advanced endpoint security

Protection well beyond a normal antivirus, built to spot suspicious behaviour and stop ransomware.

Backup and disaster recovery

Automatic, off-site copies of the data and a plan to get everything running again if a PC, a server or the whole office goes down.

For homes and gamers

Custom PCs

Built part by part around how it'll be used and the budget: gaming, work, study, graphics. New or refurbished.

Upgrades

More RAM, a fast SSD, a new graphics card or CPU. Often a slow PC can be saved for far less than a new one.

Cleaning and thermal paste

Dust out, fresh thermal paste in, for PCs and consoles that run hot and loud.

Software cleanup and remote help

Viruses and slowdowns removed without losing data, often over a remote session.
